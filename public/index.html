    How to play
    Keyboard + mouse
    WASD move · Mouse look · Left click shoot/stab · Right click aim / scope (Melee: dash) · Space jump (Melee: double jump) · Shift sprint · C / Ctrl crouch · R reload · E dash (Melee) · hold G aim grenade, release to throw · Knife: chain 3 slashes for a finisher, attack right after a dash to lunge, attack while falling to ground-slam, F throws the knife · B shop · Tab scoreboard
    Tablet
    Left thumb: move stick (push fully to sprint) · Right thumb: drag to look · Hold FIRE (drag it to aim while shooting) · AIM/DASH, JUMP, CROUCH, RELOAD buttons · hold 💣 to aim a grenade (drag to adjust), release to throw
    Goal
    Skydive in, scavenge medkits , shields , coins and gems inside buildings, spend 🪙 in the shop (B / 🛒), stay inside the shrinking zone and be the last one standing! Grab 📦 supply drops, pick up 💰 loot bags from the fallen, and hunt the 👑 bounty (the kill leader) for bonus points.
